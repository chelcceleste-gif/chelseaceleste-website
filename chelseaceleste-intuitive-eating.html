Common Questions

Before you go.

What exactly is intuitive eating — is it just "eating whatever you want"?
+
No — intuitive eating is a science-backed framework for reconnecting with your body's natural hunger and fullness cues. It's not about eating without limits; it's about eating with awareness, satisfaction, and self-trust. The guide covers this thoroughly in the first section.
Will this help me lose weight?
+
The goal of intuitive eating isn't weight loss — it's creating a sustainable, joyful relationship with food that allows your body to find its natural set point. For most people, that means feeling lighter, more energized, and more in control. Chelsea's approach is anti-diet and weight-neutral, rooted in health and wellness over numbers.
How is this different from Chelsea's Sustainable Nutrition Bundle?
+
This guide focuses specifically on the mindset and psychology of eating — the "why" behind your relationship with food. The Sustainable Nutrition Bundle covers the practical "what" — meal plans, nutrition education, and recipes. Many customers find they work beautifully together.

This guide gives you the foundation: why diets fail, how emotional eating works, and a concrete framework (the 4 Skinny Rules) to actually put intuitive eating into practice.
