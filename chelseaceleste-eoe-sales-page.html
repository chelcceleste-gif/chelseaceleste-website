Why This Bundle Exists

When my husband first showed symptoms of EoE, we were completely lost. Eosinophilic esophagitis is still a relatively new diagnosis — which means the information available is scarce, scattered, and often terrifying to wade through alone.

As a nutritionist with an MSc in Nutrition and Human Performance, I threw myself into research. This bundle is everything we wish we'd had from the beginning. Written from lived experience, grounded in nutrition science — and full of the practical guidance your doctors may not have given you.
