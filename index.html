A little about me

Science-backed.
Warm-hearted.
Real life.

I came to nutrition the hard way. In high school, a desire to be perfect led me into a disordered relationship with food that took years to untangle. That journey — from fear of food to genuine food freedom — is why I do what I do.

Today I create content, resources, and educational tools that bridge the gap between complex nutrition science and your actual, busy, beautiful life. No diet culture. No calorie counting. No guilt. Just nourishment.

What I believe

The values behind
everything I create.

01

Food is a gift

Not a punishment. Not something to fear or count or restrict. Food is medicine, pleasure, fuel, and nourishment — all at once. Every resource I create is built on that belief.

02

Science over trends

I have an MSc in Nutrition and Human Performance and I use it. Everything I recommend is rooted in real research — not whatever wellness trend landed in my inbox this week.

03

Glow from within

Your most radiant skin doesn't come from a serum — it comes from your plate. My Eat Your Skincare philosophy puts food-first, inside-out beauty at the centre of everything.

04

Perfectly imperfect

I'm a recovering perfectionist and proud of it. My content is warm, vulnerable, and honestly imperfect — because that's what real sustainable health looks like.

05

Education as empowerment

I spent 7 years as a certified teacher before becoming a nutritionist. I believe that when you truly understand why your body works the way it does, the choices become easy.

06

Real life, real women

I'm a busy mom. Every resource I create is designed to fit into your actual life — not an aspirational one with unlimited time and a personal chef.
