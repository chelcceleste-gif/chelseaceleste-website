My Recommendation Philosophy

I only recommend
what I'd tell a
friend.

With an MSc in Nutrition and years of content creation behind me, I've seen a lot of products come and go. My rule is simple: if I wouldn't genuinely recommend it to my best friend or use it myself, it doesn't make this page.

Everything here is either derm-backed, nutrition science-approved, or personally tested over months — not just sent to my door last Tuesday.

01

Personally tested

I use everything on this page. If I haven't tried it myself — on my own skin, in my own body — it doesn't make the list.

02

Science-backed

I look at ingredients, research, and formulations — not just marketing. My MSc background means I can actually read the labels.

03

Regularly updated

Products get swapped out if something better comes along. This isn't a set-it-and-forget-it list — it reflects what I'm actually using right now.

04

Honest about affiliates

Some links earn me a small commission. I disclose this clearly and it never influences what I recommend. My credibility matters more than a commission.
